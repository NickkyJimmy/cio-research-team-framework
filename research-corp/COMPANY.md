Research Corp is a hierarchical research company built as a pipeline. Work flows through specialist agents in sequence, with a Research Leader supervising every stage before handing the final output to the CEO.

1. **CEO** receives strategic research requests and delegates to the Research Leader
2. **Research Leader** supervises the full pipeline and reviews every handoff
3. **Brief + Plan** agent produces a structured research brief, then develops the research plan
4. **Questionnaire Agent** designs surveys and interview guides based on the approved plan
5. **Data Processing** agent cleans, processes, and extracts insights from collected data
6. **Report Agent** synthesizes findings into a polished, executive-ready report

The philosophy: each stage has a dedicated specialist, and the Research Leader acts as quality gate between every step. The CEO never touches execution — only receives the final product.
