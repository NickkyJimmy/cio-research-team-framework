# Research Plan Rubric

Use 0-5 for each dimension with explicit evidence.

## Scoring scale

- 5 excellent, 4 strong, 3 acceptable, 2 weak, 1 very weak, 0 missing.

## Dimensions and weights

1. Objective coverage (20%)
   - Plan content maps directly to decision and objective.
2. Method appropriateness (20%)
   - Method can answer the stated questions with valid tradeoffs.
3. Sample design quality (15%)
   - Population, quotas, and recruitment logic are coherent and feasible.
4. Instrument/analysis coherence (15%)
   - Measures and analysis approach match intended outputs.
5. Execution feasibility (15%)
   - Timeline, dependencies, staffing, and budget are realistic.
6. Risk management (15%)
   - Key risks are anticipated with practical mitigations.

## Weighted score

`weighted_points = (score / 5) * weight`

Total = sum(weighted_points) on a 0-100 scale.
