# Brief Quality Rubric

Score each dimension 0-5. Use evidence from the submitted brief.

## Scoring scale

- 5: Excellent, clear and decision-ready
- 4: Strong with minor ambiguity
- 3: Acceptable but important gaps remain
- 2: Weak, multiple blockers
- 1: Very weak, mostly unusable
- 0: Missing

## Dimensions and weights

1. Decision linkage (20%)
   - Is the business decision explicit and testable?
2. Objective clarity (20%)
   - Is the research objective specific and measurable?
3. Audience definition (15%)
   - Is target audience behaviorally defined and feasible to recruit?
4. Scope and feasibility (15%)
   - Is timeline/budget/market scope realistic?
5. Method fit (15%)
   - Is the proposed method suitable for the decision and objective?
6. Deliverable usefulness (15%)
   - Will outputs directly support the decision?

## Weighted score formula

For each dimension:

`weighted_points = (score / 5) * weight`

Total score = sum of weighted_points across all dimensions (0-100).
