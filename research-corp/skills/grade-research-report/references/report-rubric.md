# Research Report Rubric

Score each category 0-5 with direct evidence.

## Scoring scale

- 5 excellent, 4 strong, 3 acceptable, 2 weak, 1 very weak, 0 missing.

## Dimensions and weights

1. Insight clarity (20%)
   - Findings are specific, non-generic, and easy to understand.
2. Evidence strength (20%)
   - Claims are supported by credible and sufficient evidence.
3. Logic and narrative flow (15%)
   - Conclusions follow logically from findings.
4. Segment and nuance coverage (15%)
   - Differences across key groups/contexts are addressed.
5. Recommendation actionability (20%)
   - Recommendations are concrete, prioritized, and owner-ready.
6. Transparency and limitations (10%)
   - Uncertainty and limitations are disclosed responsibly.

## Weighted score

`weighted_points = (score / 5) * weight`

Total = sum(weighted_points), normalized to 0-100.
