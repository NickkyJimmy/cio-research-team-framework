## Desk Research Execution Guide (Phase 0)

Desk research is the default first phase of any research plan. It establishes what is already known before investing in primary research.

## Purpose

- Avoid duplicating research that already exists
- Provide context for questionnaire/guide design
- Identify gaps that primary research must fill
- Calibrate sample design with existing behavioral data

## What to Search For

### Internal Sources (Priority 1)
- Previous research reports on same product/audience
- Analytics data (usage funnels, cohort behavior, feature adoption)
- CRM and support ticket patterns
- A/B test results and experiment history
- Internal dashboards and KPI tracking

### Published Sources (Priority 2)
- Industry reports (Nielsen, Kantar, Statista, local equivalents)
- Competitor public filings, press releases, product updates
- Market sizing reports relevant to the category
- Academic research on the behavioral domain

### Public Data (Priority 3)
- App store reviews and ratings (own product + competitors)
- Social media sentiment and discussion patterns
- Government/regulatory data relevant to the industry
- Community forums and user discussions

## Time-Boxing

- Standard desk research: 2-3 days
- If historical data is rich: extend to 3-4 days, reduce primary research scope
- If historical data is absent: limit to 1-2 days, declare limitation

## Output Structure

### Desk Research Summary

What We Know:
- [Finding 1 + source + confidence level]
- [Finding 2 + source + confidence level]

Key Data Points:
- [Metric 1: value + source + date]
- [Metric 2: value + source + date]

Remaining Gaps:
- [Gap 1: what we still don't know + why it matters for the decision]
- [Gap 2: ...]

Implications for Primary Research:
- [How desk research findings should shape the primary research design]
- [Hypotheses strengthened or weakened by existing data]
- [Segments or questions that desk research makes more/less important]

## When Desk Research is Insufficient

If no meaningful prior data exists:
- Declare the limitation explicitly in the plan
- Consider adding a small qualitative pilot (3-5 exploratory IDIs) before full primary research
- Adjust confidence level and risk assessment accordingly
