## Objective-to-Content Mapping Reference

Use this reference to ensure each research objective is translated into measurable/explorable content and actionable reporting sections.

## Mapping Rule

- Each sub-objective must map to at least one content block.
- Each content block must support at least one sub-objective.
- Each content block must include explicit decision use.

## Content Block Template

Content Block: [name]
- Maps to sub-objective(s): [obj_1, obj_2]
- Key questions: [question set]
- Measures/dimensions: [metrics + dimensions]
- Question formats: [scale/open-end/MC/ranking]
- Analysis approach: [cross-tab/regression/thematic coding]
- Decision use: [what action this enables]

## Objective Pattern Library

### 1) Awareness / Campaign Effectiveness
- Measures: aided awareness, unaided awareness, recall, message comprehension
- Analysis: segment cross-tab, asset-level comparison
- Decision use: media allocation, creative refinement

### 2) Funnel Conversion
- Measures: awareness -> consideration -> trial -> usage -> repeat
- Analysis: drop-off decomposition, segment deltas
- Decision use: funnel optimization priorities

### 3) Churn / Retention
- Measures: churn reasons, exit trigger timing, reactivation motivators
- Analysis: driver ranking, cohort comparisons, thematic synthesis
- Decision use: retention intervention roadmap

### 4) Product Concept Evaluation
- Measures: concept clarity, relevance, uniqueness, adoption intent
- Analysis: concept scorecard + qualitative diagnostics
- Decision use: go/no-go and concept iteration

### 5) Pricing Research
- Measures: willingness to pay, price sensitivity (Van Westendorp / Gabor-Granger), price-value perception
- Analysis: price-demand curve, segment-level price elasticity, competitive price benchmarking
- Decision use: pricing strategy, tier structure, promotional pricing

### 6) UX / Usability Evaluation
- Measures: task completion rate, time on task, error rate, satisfaction (SUS/CSAT), ease of use
- Analysis: task-level performance matrix, heuristic issue severity, qualitative friction mapping
- Decision use: UX prioritization roadmap, feature redesign priorities

### 7) Brand Health Tracking
- Measures: brand awareness (aided/unaided), brand attributes, brand consideration, NPS, brand loyalty index
- Analysis: brand funnel tracking, attribute mapping vs competitors, trend over time
- Decision use: brand positioning refinement, communication strategy

### 8) Market Sizing / Demand Estimation
- Measures: current demand (usage incidence x frequency x value), latent demand (intent among non-users), addressable market share
- Analysis: top-down vs bottom-up sizing, segment contribution, demand sensitivity modeling
- Decision use: market entry decision, investment sizing, growth forecasting

## Traceability Check

Before finalizing plan, verify:

- No orphan objective (every sub-objective has >= 1 content block)
- No orphan content block (every block maps to >= 1 sub-objective)
- Report sections align 1:1 with content blocks
- Every key business decision has an evidence path
