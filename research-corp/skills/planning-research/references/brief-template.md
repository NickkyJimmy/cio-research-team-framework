## Research Brief Template & Validation Guide

Use this reference to validate incoming briefs and identify gaps before planning begins.

## Required Fields

Every brief must contain these fields. If missing, request before proceeding.

### 1. Business Context
- Product/service name
- Current performance (metrics, baselines)
- Business direction or strategic intent

### 2. Business Objective
- What business outcome is this research supporting?
- What KPI should move if findings are acted on?

### 3. Decision Framework
- Decision owner (name + role)
- Decision deadline
- What will change based on findings?
- What are the options being evaluated?

### 4. Target Audience
- Who is the research about? (even if rough)
- Known segments or user types
- Any exclusions

### 5. Budget
- Budget range or envelope
- Or explicit confirmation that budget is flexible

### 6. Timeline
- Desired completion date
- Fixed deadlines vs flexible timing
- Dependencies (launches, campaigns, board reviews)

## Recommended Fields

These strengthen the brief significantly. Fill from context if the brief does not provide them.

### 7. Existing Information
- Prior research on this topic
- Internal data available (analytics, CRM, support tickets)
- Key insights already known

### 8. Desired Information
- Specific gaps to fill
- Questions that existing data cannot answer

### 9. Hypotheses
- What does the team currently believe?
- What assumptions should be tested?

### 10. Actions Based on Findings
- If hypothesis A is confirmed, what action?
- If hypothesis B is rejected, what alternative?

### 11. Research Context
- Product research type: new product / improve existing / sunset evaluation
- User reach: % of user base accessible digitally
- Competitive landscape: known competitors, market position

### 12. Method Preferences (optional)
- Stakeholder method preference (if any)
- Note: objective fit always overrides preference

## Brief Quality Scoring

Rate the brief before proceeding:

- High confidence: all required fields present, KPI explicit, decision owner named, hypotheses stated
- Medium confidence: objective clear but KPI vague or decision framework incomplete
- Low confidence: vague objective, no decision framing, unclear audience

Document the score and any assumptions made to fill gaps.
