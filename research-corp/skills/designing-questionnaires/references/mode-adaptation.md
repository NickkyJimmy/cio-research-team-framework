# Mode Adaptation Guide

Adapt instrument format and constraints to the target data collection mode. Each mode has specific limitations that affect question types, length, and format.

---

## CASI / Online Survey

- **Maximum duration:** 20–25 minutes
- Complex routing and skip logic fully supported
- Grid/matrix acceptable (limit 7–10 items per grid)
- Multimedia stimuli supported (images, video, audio)
- Open-ends: limit 3–5 per survey to reduce drop-off
- **Mobile optimization:** single-column layout, large tap targets, no horizontal grids

---

## CATI (Phone Interview)

- **Maximum duration:** 15–20 minutes
- Simplify scales (max 5 points, read all labels aloud)
- Reduce open-ends (max 2–3)
- No visual stimuli possible
- Include interviewer instructions in brackets: "[Interviewer: read...]"
- Add verbal transition prompts, e.g.: *"Tôi sẽ đọc cho bạn một số lựa chọn..."*

---

## Field Survey (Face-to-face)

- **Maximum duration:** 20–30 minutes
- Use show cards for complex scales and long option lists
- Include show card references in brackets: "[Show card: ...]"
- Interviewer can probe and clarify when needed

---

## Mobile In-app Survey

- **Maximum duration:** 3–5 minutes (under 10 questions)
- Supported question types only: single-select, multi-select, scale (1–5)
- No or minimal open-ends (1 optional at most)
- No grids, matrices, or complex routing
- Keep response options short — they must fit on a mobile screen

---

## IDI / FGD (Qualitative Modes)

- Use discussion guide format (not questionnaire format)
- Include moderator instructions and probing cues throughout
- Mark optional sections: "[OPTIONAL — if time permits]"
- Include stimulus handling instructions where applicable

### Timing Guide

- **IDI:** 45–60 minutes total (75 minutes maximum)
- **FGD:** 90–120 minutes total (120 minutes maximum)
- Allocate time per section proportional to objective priority
